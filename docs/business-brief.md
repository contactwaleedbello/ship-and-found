# Business brief: DG Tech Store

## Confirmed business context

DG Tech Store is a smartphone and laptop shop at Shop ATF 03, New Baccab Plaza (Old Kasupda Building), Ahmadu Bello Way. Primary customers are students; workers and traders also buy devices, including resellers. Aged 20–35. Returning customers are important.

The shop sells smartphones (Apple iPhone, Google Pixel, Samsung, Redmi and other brands) and laptops (Dell, HP, Apple MacBook, Lenovo and other brands); it is not limited to those brands.

Customers usually contact the shop on WhatsApp or phone before visiting. The three most frequent questions are: Is the product available? What is the current price? Does it have good battery life?

Battery life matters because electricity is inconsistent, and some customers avoid carrying power banks. Product information should make battery specifications easy to find without inventing performance claims.

Observed pattern: new students often start with lower-end laptops, then return for higher-performance models as needs develop. This is not universal; support entry-level, mid-range, higher-performance and upgrading customers.

English and Hausa are the primary languages. A Yoruba-speaking co-owner receives orders in Yoruba. The interface is English; full multilingual support is not claimed or tested. Typography preserves Hausa and Yoruba glyph coverage.

Connectivity is generally average. Customers are comfortable with smartphones and laptops. The interface should load reasonably and stay responsive without being over-simplified.

Prices change slightly with exchange rates. The system should support price updates and avoid implying prices are permanently fixed.

Warranty is two days: faulty devices must be returned within that period; physical damage is excluded. Part payment is not accepted. Make these conditions visible where relevant.

Contact is by WhatsApp and phone. Both should be prominent actions. The phone number must not be exposed as public text.

## Desired experience

The owner's desired experience is: trusting, fast and reliable. Trust comes from clear policies and accurate information; speed from easy access to availability, price and battery details; reliability from current information and a contact route.

The one thing that must work: customers can confirm availability, price and battery details, then contact the shop on WhatsApp or phone.

## Design implications

Prioritise availability, price and battery in product discovery. Price displays need a "confirm current price" path. Product data must support brands, categories, entry-to-high-performance tiers and returning-customer upgrades. Contact, warranty and payment actions must be visible. Preserve Hausa/Yoruba glyph coverage without claiming a multilingual interface that does not exist.
