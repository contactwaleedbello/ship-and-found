Business brief: DG Tech-Store (Gadget Plug)

DG Tech-Store is a phone shop at Shop ATF 03, New Bacab Plaza (the old KASUPDA building), Ahmadu Bello Way. 
 They sell phones, laptops, acessories to customers who either goes to them or reaches out to them on their whatsapp number or call line, they opperate directly with customers or retailers, in other to acheive their sales and target of serving every customer.

Most of their customers are students. Workers come next, and then traders who resell. They are between 20 and 35 years old.

Customers reach the owner by call or WhatsApp. Most of them message or call before they visit the shop. Their questions are mostly about availability and price [add the third question the owner named].

The owner has simple rules. A faulty device can be returned within two days. The warranty does not cover physical damage. The shop does not take part payments for the devices and accessories sold. Product prices change from time to time, but only by small amounts because of the exchange rate mostly.

The owner describes the brand with three words: trusting, fast and reliable.
