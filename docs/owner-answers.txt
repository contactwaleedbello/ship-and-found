1. Business identity
- Name: DG Tech Store.
- Location: Shop ATF 03, New Baccab Plaza (Old Kasupda Building),
  Ahmadu Bello Way.

2. Customers
- Primary customers: students.
- Other customers: workers and traders, including traders who buy devices
  for resale.
- Typical age range: 20–35.
- Returning customers are important to the business.

3. Product categories
- Smartphones: Apple iPhone, Google Pixel, Samsung, Redmi and other
  smartphone brands.
- Laptops: Dell, HP, Apple MacBook, Lenovo and other laptop brands.
- The business sells smartphones and laptops generally; it is not restricted
  to the named brands.

4. Customer behaviour and common questions
- Customers commonly contact the store through WhatsApp or phone calls
  before visiting.
- Three most frequent questions:
  1. Is the product available?
  2. What is the current price?
  3. Does it have good battery life?
- Battery life matters because electricity is not consistently available
  everywhere and some customers do not want to carry power banks.
- Product information should make battery specifications easy to find, but
  must not invent battery performance claims.

5. Purchasing progression (observed pattern, not a universal rule)
- New university students commonly begin with lower-end laptops because
  these meet their initial needs and budgets.
- As their academic and professional needs develop, they may return to buy
  higher-performance laptops.
- The discovery experience should support entry-level, mid-range,
  higher-performance and upgrade customers.

6. Languages
- English and Hausa are the primary communication languages.
- A Yoruba-speaking co-owner receives orders in Yoruba.
- This does not confirm that a complete three-language website is required.
  The current interface is English; full multilingual interface support is
  not claimed or tested.

7. Connectivity and technical comfort
- Customer internet connectivity is generally average.
- Customers are comfortable using smart devices.
- Customers are also comfortable using laptops.
- Design for reasonable loading performance and responsive behaviour without
  unnecessarily simplifying the interface.

8. Pricing
- Prices sometimes change slightly because of exchange-rate movements.
- The system should accommodate price updates without implying that prices
  are permanently fixed.

9. Warranty and payment policy
- Warranty period: two days.
- Faulty devices must be returned within that period.
- Physical damage is excluded from warranty coverage.
- Part payment is not accepted.
- These conditions should be communicated clearly wherever relevant to the
  customer journey.

10. Contact channels
- WhatsApp.
- Phone calls.
- Both should be presented as prominent clickable links or actions.
- The phone number must not be exposed as ordinary public-facing text in the
  repository or interface.

11. Desired experience
- The owner's exact desired experience is: TRUSTING, FAST AND RELIABLE.
