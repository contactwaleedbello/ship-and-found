Result: The rate limit was successfully bypassed. The application keyed solely on IP address; by changing the network egress point, the attacker received a fresh 5-request allocation.

--- 3. Architectural Defense Analysis ---
This experiment conclusively proves why IP-based rate limiting alone is insufficient. Serious production applications must key on multi-dimensional tuples:
1. IP Address + Targeted Account Email/Phone (Composite Key)
2. Device & Hardware Fingerprinting (JA3/TLS fingerprinting, browser canvas hash)
3. Cryptographic Proof-of-Work / CAPTCHA challenge on threshold exceeded
