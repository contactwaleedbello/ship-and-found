1. DEFECT DESCRIPTION & CONDITIONS

"Samsung Galaxy A15 (128GB) Takardar Biyan Kudi Kashi-Kashi (Part-Payment)"

3. Visual Weight Destruction (FR-01 Violation): When heading clipped, the dynamic price (₦235,000) dropped below the fold or displaced the Battery Endurance badge ("5000 mAh (18h Video)"), destroying the mandatory equal-prominence visual contract.
